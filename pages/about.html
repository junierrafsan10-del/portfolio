About Me

Learn more about my background, clients, and expertise.

I'm a web developer and ICT teacher based in Dhaka. Over the past 12 years I've worked with schools, small businesses, and nonprofits across Bangladesh - building websites, teaching students, and helping organisations establish their digital presence. My journey began with graphic design and gradually expanded into full-stack web development and digital strategy.

I believe that great work comes from listening carefully, asking the right questions, and collaborating closely with clients. Every project is a partnership, and my goal is always to make my clients look good by delivering work that is both effective and beautiful.

When I'm not working, you'll find me experimenting with new technologies, mentoring junior designers, or exploring the outdoors. I'm based in Dhaka but work with clients around the world.
